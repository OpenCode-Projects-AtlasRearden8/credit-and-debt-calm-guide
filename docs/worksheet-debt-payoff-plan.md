## Step 5 — the two standing questions, once a month

- [ ] Did any emptied card refill, even a little? (If yes: move that card somewhere harder to reach — the drawer, the file cabinet, the back of the freezer — before it does it again.)
- [ ] Did the *extra* number dip? Payday money leaves first; month's-end leftover is a rumor.

## The honest fine print of consolidation

A lower monthly payment with a longer term can mean *more* total interest. Before approving any consolidation or balance transfer:

- [ ] I divided the *total interest over the new loan* against the current path, not just the monthly payment.
- [ ] The emptied card(s) get a boring job or a locked drawer, whichever is harder to undo.

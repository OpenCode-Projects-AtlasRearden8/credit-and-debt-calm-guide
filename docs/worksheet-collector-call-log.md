## Before answering, three cards face up on the table

- I don't acknowledge or admit anything on a call I didn't ask for.
- I don't promise or pay anything today — *especially* on an old debt (partial payments can restart the clock; your state AG's site keeps the local answer).
- I get everything in writing. Record-keeping is quiet leverage — it's what this page is for.

## After any call with rules broken (call hour, tones, workplace calls, threats)

- [ ] Entered in the log above, words as close to theirs as I can manage.
- [ ] Certified "stop contacting me" letter mailed, if calls continue or the tone stays hostile.
- [ ] [CFPB complaint](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/complaint/) filed free — case number: ____________
- [ ] State Attorney General consumer office notified if the pattern continues.

## If they actually send the validation notice

- [ ] 30-day dispute window is running: validation request letter ([template](dispute-letter-templates.md)) mailed within it, receipt kept.
- [ ] Names and dates get checked against my own records (and my [dispute tracker](worksheet-dispute-tracker.md), which runs in parallel) before anything is paid or promised.
